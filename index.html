Background

Credit and pricing product SME with 20+ years in financial services and 12 years owning a commercial profitability pricing platform end to end. The deal pricing engine evaluates $400M+ in annual deal volume — underwriting logic, credit risk models, loan economics, and multi-product deal structures spanning bundled pricing, scenario analysis, profitability projection versus P&L, and portfolio optimization. The models kept evolving with users, markets, products, and regulation, and so did the platform.

Deep in RAROC, Expected Loss, Fund Transfer Pricing, and cost-of-capital methodology — the same mathematical patterns that apply directly to consumer products. Currently completing the IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate, covering machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI.

Why AI

Pink Analytica believes AI is a revolutionary technology, and is embracing it fully. There is enormous potential for good in it, and the goal is to be part of the team that gets things done and makes it real.

That curiosity about technology traces back to a math undergraduate degree and a Business Analytics master's — a drive to understand how a system actually works, not just what it outputs. Pink Analytica isn't watching this shift from the sidelines. It builds executive dashboards with it, accelerates SQL, Python, and VBA work with it, and uses it daily inside a production pricing platform — backed by an in-progress IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate, going deeper into machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. This isn't about advising on transformation from the outside. It's about being on the team that builds it.

In practice that means the unglamorous work: implementing new systems, reorganizing workflows around them so people actually adopt them, embedding AI agents into everyday processes, and taking the legacy technology everyone is afraid to touch and turning it into something that works.
